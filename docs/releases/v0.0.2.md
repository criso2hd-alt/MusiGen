# MusiGen v0.0.2 — The Neon Boutique

A new way to explore your music, plus improvements throughout the studio.

## New features
- Embedded first-person 3D vinyl store with a Blender-authored interior, neon lighting, entry/exit light transitions, and a live back-wall audio visualizer.
- Pick up and rotate sleeves, inspect track details, and play records on an animated turntable with spinning vinyl and a moving tonearm.
- Audio/MIDI reference import, microphone recording, and MIDI keyboard capture with audible monitoring. Optional media-tool installation and repair from Setup.
- Searchable/customizable ingredients, adjustable text size, Pair and Shake up suggestions, and a collapsible queue inside Mixer.
- System-resource monitoring, saved generation recipes/seeds, automatic song titles, and duration-aware lyric writing.
- Improved library cards and playlist playback, remembered visualizer presets, lyric appearance controls, and optional word-timing synchronization.

See the [README screenshots and controls](https://github.com/criso2hd-alt/MusiGen#new-in-v002).

## Install or update
1. Download **MusiGen.zip** below.
2. For a new installation, extract the MusiGen folder somewhere writable and run **MusiGen.exe**. First launch installs its private runtime; choose models in Setup.
3. To update, **close MusiGen**, then copy the new **MusiGen.exe** into your existing app folder, replacing the old EXE. Keep your **models, runtime, data, and music** folders intact.

The ZIP contains only the executable, README, and license. Models and optional tools are downloaded separately. SHA256SUMS.txt provides the ZIP checksum.

## Requirements and limits
- Windows 11 and an NVIDIA GPU; 16 GB VRAM recommended for YuE2.
- The 3D store is available while heavy AI operations are idle, with Balanced and Low graphics options.
- Reference arrangements guide generation; exact melody reproduction, vocals, and final duration are not guaranteed. Durations above 240 seconds remain experimental.
- Recognized lyric timing may need correction. Without word timing, scrolling is approximate.
- Model weights have separate licenses; YuE2 weights are non-commercial.
