# MusiGen v0.0.5 — Sheet Music

For the musicians in our Discord who asked to read, play and rebuild the songs MusiGen makes.

## New: Sheet Music view
Songs generated with chain-of-thought on (the default) include a written score. Click **Score** on a Library card or in the full-screen player to open it.

- **Real notation.** Vocal and instrument staves, chord symbols, key, meter and tempo, laid out in systems you can read at the piano.
- **Follow.** Highlights the notes as the song plays and scrolls with them. Seek, pause and play stay in sync.
- **Print / PDF.** Prints just the score, black on white, without splitting a system across pages. Choose "Save as PDF" in the print dialog to keep a copy.
- **MIDI export.** Saves a multi-track `.mid` (tempo, vocal melody, instrument melody, chords) to open in any DAW or notation program.
- **ABC export.** Saves the score as ABC text for MuseScore, EasyABC and other ABC tools.

Files are saved through the normal Windows Save dialog.

## Good to know
- The score is the musical plan the model writes before it sings. It matches the song's key, tempo, chords and form, and usually its melody, but the audio can drift from it in places. Treat it as a strong lead sheet rather than an exact transcription.
- Songs made with chain-of-thought off have no score. The Score button explains this.
- Editing the score or MIDI inside MusiGen is planned for a later release.

## Install / update
Download **MusiGen.zip**. For an existing installation, close MusiGen and replace only **MusiGen.exe**, preserving your **models**, **runtime**, **python**, **data** and **music** folders. New users can extract the folder and run the EXE. Nothing new is downloaded on first launch.

The ZIP contains only MusiGen.exe, README.txt and LICENSE. It contains no models, runtime, library database or personal music. SHA256SUMS.txt provides the download checksum.

Coming from v0.0.3 or earlier? v0.0.4 fixed Romanian and other non-Latin lyrics, made the runtime fully portable (no dependency on drive C:), made instrumentals fill the requested length and added "Clear finished" to the queue. See the [v0.0.4 notes](v0.0.4.md).
